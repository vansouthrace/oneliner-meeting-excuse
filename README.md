Entry for the [The Funniest Excuse for Skipping a Meeting](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mfnakmal-the-funniest-excuse-for-skipping-a-meeting-5cecf163) bounty on make.fun: one razor-sharp line that gets you out of a pointless meeting (max 200 characters).

> Can't make it — my calendar and I had a difficult conversation, and it decided this meeting isn't aligned with my priorities this quarter.

## How to run

```sh
node main.ts
```

This validates the line against the gate (1–200 characters, single line, not only whitespace) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this line

The field is crowded with recursive jokes — a meeting about cutting meetings, a meeting invented to avoid the meeting, a cancellation meeting. This one uses a different mechanism entirely: corporate HR-speak turned against the meeting itself. "My calendar and I had a difficult conversation" personifies the calendar, and "isn't aligned with my priorities this quarter" is the exact euphemism every office worker has heard in a performance review. It is relatable, it needs no setup, it reads like an excuse someone would actually send, and the punch lands on the last words.
